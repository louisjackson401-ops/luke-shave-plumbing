Cookie Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information when you use this website.

Last updated: 24 July 2026

About Cookies

Cookies are small text files a website may store on your device. This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on the Luke Shave Plumbing & Bathrooms website.

Cookies We Use

This website does not set any first-party tracking, analytics or advertising cookies of its own.

Third-Party Services

Some embedded services may set cookies or receive technical data such as your IP address when a page loads:

  • Google Maps (the embedded map) and Google Fonts — provided by Google, which may set cookies under its own policies.
  • FormSubmit — delivers enquiry-form submissions to our inbox; it does not set tracking cookies in your browser.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the website.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect how embedded features such as the map display.

More Information

For how we handle personal data you submit through our forms, please see our Cookie Policy.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
